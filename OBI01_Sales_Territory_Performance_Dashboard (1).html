Methodology Note — Returns & Refunds

Approved_Refund_Amount is the recorded field value. It is not confirmed as a settled financial liability without supporting ledger evidence.

Quantity-Based Refund Benchmark = Original Order Line Net Amount × Approved Return Quantity ÷ Original Sold Quantity, capped at the original line amount. This is an analytical proxy applied only to returns whose original order falls within the provisional recognised sales cohort (Delivered + Delivered).

Data quality contradiction: . No credit note issuance is assumed from these records without independent ledger confirmation.

Provisional Recognised Sales

Only order lines where Order_Status = "Delivered" AND Delivery_Status = "Delivered" are included. All other statuses (Pending, Partially Delivered, Cancelled) are excluded from revenue recognition.

Refund Benchmark Calculation

  • Only Approved returns are eligible for the benchmark deduction.
  • Benchmark = Original Line Net Amount × Return_Quantity ÷ Original_Quantity (capped at original line amount).
  • Only returns whose Original_Order_ID falls within the recognised cohort are deducted from that cohort's sales.
  • Pending and Rejected returns are not deducted.

Target Reporting Month

Month_ID from Fact_Monthly_Sales_Targets is used as the reporting month key. Month_Name contains an incorrect year and is used for display only.

Sales & Target Aggregation

Sales and targets are aggregated independently to the same territory-month grain before joining, to avoid fact-to-fact join fan-out.

Gross Profit

Recorded gross profit is shown at order-line level as supplied. It is presented before return-cost adjustments and is not presented as return-adjusted profit.

Customer Activities

There is no Linked_Order_ID field connecting customer activities to specific sales orders. Activity counts and outcomes are reported separately. No verified visit-to-order conversion rate is claimed.

Delivery

There is no Delivered_Quantity field in the supplied data. Actual delivered quantities and delivery completion percentages are not shown.

Returns Workflow Data Quality

96 of 320 return records have Return_Status of "Pending" or "Rejected" but Resolution = "Credit Note Issued". This is a data-quality or workflow contradiction. No credit note issuance is assumed without supporting ledger evidence.

Synthetic Data Disclosure

All company names, customer names, product records, transaction data and territory records are synthetically generated for portfolio demonstration purposes. No actual commercial organisation, trading relationship or financial position is represented.

Assumptions & Limitations

  • Target data covers 33 territories × 24 months = 792 target records. All territories have targets for the full period.
  • One customer (CUS-0132) has no recognised sales in the reporting period.
  • Returns reference original order lines but no Join to delivery records exists; approved refund amounts are as filed.
  • Sales representatives are assigned to primary territories; some territories have multiple reps.
  • Gross profit is recorded at line level but no COGS adjustment for returns is available.
